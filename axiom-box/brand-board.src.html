Channel identity · v1 · Oct 2026

Axiom Box

Merch graphic · the name as a puzzle
AXE+ION−N+M=AXIOMBOX

Two marks share one look. The cube with the zigzag crack is the channel logo: avatar, watermark, end screens. The rebus spells the name out for merch and banners. The name is a puzzle you solve with pictures. Every piece of the channel should feel the same way: a bold flat icon, a bit of arithmetic, and a payoff in cream and teal on black.

03 · Texture

Print finish

Every solid fill carries a fine black speckle, as if the ink didn't fully cover. It makes flat vector shapes read as printed. Keep it inside shapes. The black ground stays clean.

04 · Rules

Graphic style

Do

  • Draw icons as flat shapes with a thick teal outline and black inner linework.
  • Build new graphics as puzzles: an object, a sign, a letter. Plus, minus, equals.
  • Keep each icon to two inks plus black.
  • Leave generous black space around the logo, at least the height of the "M".
  • Use the full rebus where it can be read. Below 200 px wide, use the cube logo instead.

Don't

  • Put the logo on white or light grey. The cream ink and black linework disappear.
  • Let orange sit directly against teal.
  • Add gradients, glows, drop shadows or 3D bevels.
  • Redraw or rearrange the cube. Its hexagon, inner edges and zigzag crack stay as drawn.
  • Swap the colors of "AXIOM" and "BOX", or set the wordmark in a thin weight.
  • Speckle the black background.
